## ?? **Key Points**

### **Who Are the Players:**

1. **Hospital User ("nawya")**
   - From: `dbo.Users` table
   - Role: User (Hospital staff)
   - Email: nawya.godasi@gmail.com
   - Action: **REQUESTS** blood

2. **Blood Banks (City Blood Bank, etc.)**
   - From: `dbo.Hospitals` table
   - Has: Blood inventory/stock
   - Email: city@bloodbank.com
   - Action: **PROVIDES** blood

3. **Donors (Optional)**
   - From: `dbo.Donors` table
   - Role: People who donate blood
   - Action: Donate to blood banks

## ?? **Matching Logic**

Blood banks are notified if:
```
? Blood Group matches (A+ = A+, B+ = B+, etc.)
? Stock available >= units requested
? Blood bank is active (IsActive = true)
? Blood bank has email address
```

Example:
```
Request: A+, 2 units

Blood Bank Stock:
  APos = 50 ? 50 >= 2 ? MATCH
  BPos = 30 ? Not A+ ? NO MATCH
```
